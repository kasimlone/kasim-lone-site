Task 2 Calculating download time / 10 marks

Use the Calculating download time section. Show all working — mark up each answer with what you divided and what unit you converted.

Unit reminder:
8 bits (b) = 1 byte (B)
1,000 bytes = 1 kilobyte (kB)
1,000,000 bytes = 1 megabyte (MB)
1,000,000,000 bytes = 1 gigabyte (GB)
To convert Mb/s → MB/s, divide by 8.
Method (from the worked example):
  1. Step 1 — Convert the bandwidth from bits/s into bytes/s (÷ 8).
  2. Step 2 — Divide the file size by that speed. Answer = time in seconds.
  3. Check the units match before you divide (both must be in bytes, or both in bits).
